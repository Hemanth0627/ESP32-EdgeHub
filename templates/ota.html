How it works

  1. In Arduino IDE choose Sketch → Export Compiled Binary to get a .bin file.
  2. Drop the file on the upload box (or click it) and press Upload Firmware.
  3. EdgeHub sends it to the ESP32, which flashes it and reboots. Keep the board powered.
